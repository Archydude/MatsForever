# Mats Forever

Which professions use a crafting material, right on its tooltip.

Hover any crafting material and Mats Forever adds a line naming every profession that uses it, with its icon, and how many of its recipes need it. Example, on Linen Cloth: `Mat For: Engineering (6), Blacksmithing (3), First Aid (2), Tailoring (1)`

- **Your own professions come first**, in green, so you can tell at a glance whether to keep a material or sell it. The **Only my professions** option hides the rest.
- **Every profession counts**, not just the ones your character knows: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Mining (smelting), rogue Poisons, and the new Herbalism, Skinning and Fishing crafts.
- **Recipes you haven't learned count too**, including the recipes new to WoW Forever.

## Hold Shift for the recipes

While you hold **Shift**, the tooltip lists each profession's recipes that use the material, easiest first, with how many of it each one takes next to its name, and its skill levels in the recipe colors on the right:

- **Orange**: the skill the recipe needs. Shown for recipes learned from a pattern, plan, schematic or recipe item; the game doesn't publish what trainers ask for.
- **Yellow, green, grey**: where the recipe turns yellow, green and grey, so you can see how long it gives skill-ups.

Example, on Linen Cloth:

```
Engineering
    Rough Dynamite x1           30 45 60
    EZ-Thro Dynamite x1         100 115 122 130
```

Professions with a long list show their first 10 recipes and how many more there are.

## Options

Esc > Options > AddOns > Mats Forever, or type `/matsforever`:

- **Show on tooltips**: turn the line off without disabling the addon.
- **Hold Shift for recipes**: turn off the recipe list if you use Shift for something else.
- **Only my professions**: leave out professions this character doesn't have. Materials none of your professions use get no line.

## Good to know

- Recipes taught by trainers show no orange number: the game doesn't publish the skill trainers ask for. Their yellow number is usually a little above it.
- Profession names follow your game's language. Recipe names come from the game, so they're in your language too.
- After a big WoW Forever update that adds recipes, the addon may need an update to show them.

Works on **WoW Forever**.

## Install

Copy the `MatsForever` folder to `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` and restart the game.

## Use

Hover a material anywhere: bags, bank, the auction house, mail, loot windows, chat links and recipe windows.
